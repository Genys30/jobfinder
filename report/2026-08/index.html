How to read this

  • Open roles = postings live on the boards we track that day (about 100 sources, LinkedIn included in the daily totals). It is a stock of vacancies, not hires.
  • Shares are each group's part of the day's open roles, averaged over the month. They do not add up to 100%: a role can be unclassified, and role groups come from the title alone.
  • The total grows when we add sources, so compare the mix, not the raw count, across months.
  • A jump of several points between two months more often means a new source or an update to how titles are classified than a real market shift. Treat single-month moves with care.
  • "Not tracked" means the classifier did not cover that group yet in that month.
  • Current month figures are month to date and will change until the month ends.
